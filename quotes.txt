## Mr. Jeremiah Thistlewood
Politics: what a toxic endeavour, yet the air we breathe.
My life is mine; let my art be yours.
Decency demands distance.
My hair turned white from the gambling addiction. But it grew back from the drinks.

## Thistlewood, stage directions
He raises his tankard to you and spills most of it.
He bows, very nearly to the floor, and stays there a while.
He taps his nose twice, as if you now share a secret.
He forgets you are there and says it again, more quietly.

## The fountain
It lands face up. The house considers that a good sign.
A koi you had not noticed takes an interest, then thinks better of it.
Somewhere above, a window lights up. Coincidence, probably.
The water keeps it. The water keeps everything.
It skips twice and sinks beside a much older chip.

## The hotel lifts
The Grand Hotel opens later. The lift attendant is asleep on his stool.
